# Look and script structure (the "History Vanguard" style)

Look (engine does this automatically): illustrated parchment strategy map with relief, stamped tree icons, curved sea/river
labels; tinted realms with rough inked borders that GROW from the capital; tapered campaign arrows; portrait medallions
that pop in when a name is spoken (noor light only for the Prophet and the Sahaba, crest when no likeness exists); year badge;
roman-numeral chapter cards; battle pills with two shields; real-image plates with torn edges and Ken Burns; word-synced
source-quote card; tactical close-ups (walled cities, camps, tents, horses, fires, smoke, burning); drop-in intro; grade,
vignette, sharpen. Long timelines: the MAP STYLE AGES with the era (ancient -> medieval -> safavid -> qajar -> pahlavi ->
modern), dissolving at chapter cards.

Script (~130 wpm; 1 min ~ 130-160 words; 20 min ~ 2,600 words)
- Cold open: "<Place>, <date>." + a vivid scene, then the stakes / the question.
- "Chapter one. <Title>." spoken at each chapter start (cue for the card).
- Academic but vivid; concrete numbers, places, names; primary sources quoted (chronicles, inscriptions).
- End with a chronicle quote, a consequence, or a cliffhanger.
- Titles: "X vs Y | Battle (Year)" or "The Rise and Fall of X". Thumbnail: map left, staring painted face right
  (not for religious figures), 2-4 words of drama text, cold palette, no emoji.
